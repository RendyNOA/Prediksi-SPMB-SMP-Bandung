Admission flow: how many chances?

There are two selection rounds, each with 2 school choices. Failing Round 1 does not block Round 2. Students admitted in Round 1 must re-register and do not join Round 2; not re-registering counts as withdrawing.

How the 2026 system works

Registration runs in two rounds. Round 1 (2nd week of June): merit and affirmative tracks. Students who are not admitted may join Round 2 (late June): domicile and parent-transfer tracks. The usual strategy is to try the merit track first and keep domicile as the safety net.

On to senior high: does the junior high choice matter?

Senior high admission (SMA) is run by West Java Province, not the city. Its public 2026 data lists only name, track, score, and sub-district; the applicant's junior high is not published, so "graduates of school X go to school Y" cannot be computed from official data. The senior high report-card track ignores school name

Official 2026 formula: 50% junior high report average over 5 semesters + 50% junior high TKA. No class-rank bonus and no school weighting. A junior high matters only through the grades a student earns there and how well it prepares them for the TKA.

Method, assumptions, and limitations
  • Data: Recapitulation page (min–max score/distance per track, 2023–2025), School Info (2026 quotas and applicants), and Round 1 & 2 selection results 2026 (score, TKA, and distance of admitted students, aggregated only).
  • Report card: the student's score uses the 2026 formula. TKA uncertainty is assumed at ±7 points. For selective schools, next year's cut-off is modeled as the 2026 cut-off ±1.8 points (matching 2023–2025 volatility). Schools that accepted unranked students in 2026 carry a risk of turning selective again, based on their history.
  • Domicile: next year's distance cut-off is modeled as log-normal, weighting 2026 at 55%, 2025 at 25%, 2024 at 15%, and 2023 at 5%. Some newer schools swing sharply between years.
  • Report average: simple mean of 8 subjects (religion, Pancasila, Indonesian, mathematics, science & social, arts & crafts, physical education, English) across 5 semesters: grades 4 and 5 plus grade 6 semester 1. Empty cells are skipped.
  • TKA: the elementary TKA certificate lists Indonesian and Mathematics separately. Admitted 2026 TKA scores always end in .33 / .67 / .00, consistent with averaging two scores in steps of 3.33, so the calculator averages the two.
  • School quality: no official per-school quality data is public (the national exam was abolished and school report cards are not public). Cut-off scores and demand measure selectivity, not teaching quality. Visit the schools.
  • Next year's rules may change (quotas, TKA weight, schedule). Watch spmb.bandung.go.id from around April; in 2026, account registration opened in mid-May.
